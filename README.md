# 💸 PaisaFlow

### Your Money, Under Control.

PaisaFlow is a personal finance management web app designed to help you track your income, expenses, transfers, account balances and fuel expenses in one place.

It provides a clean, modern interface that makes managing everyday finances simple and organized.

## ✨ Features

### 🏠 Dashboard
- View your total balance at a glance.
- Quick access to common financial actions.
- View recent transactions.
- Monitor your financial activity.

### 💰 Transaction Management
- Add income and expenses.
- Record transfers between Cash Wallet and Bank Account.
- Edit and delete transactions.
- Organize transactions by category.
- Filter transactions by type and date.
- Sort transactions by newest, oldest or amount.

### 🏦 Account Management
- Separate Cash Wallet and Bank Account details.
- View account-specific transaction history.
- Track total received and total spent.
- Monitor money transferred in and out.
- Hide or show account balances.

### ⛽ Fuel Tracker
- Record petrol refill amounts.
- Calculate fuel quantity automatically using the price per litre.
- Track odometer readings.
- View fuel-related history and statistics.

### 📊 Financial Insights
- View income and expense summaries.
- Analyze spending patterns.
- Explore category-wise expense breakdowns.
- Monitor your financial activity through visual insights.

### 🎯 Budget Management
- Set a monthly spending limit.
- Keep track of your spending against your budget.

### 🔒 Privacy & Convenience
- Hide or show sensitive balance information.
- Save privacy preferences locally.
- Smooth tab navigation and interface animations.
- Responsive mobile-first design.

## 🎨 Design

PaisaFlow focuses on a modern, minimal and mobile-friendly experience.

- Midnight dark theme.
- Green accent colors.
- Clean financial dashboard.
- Rounded cards and subtle borders.
- Smooth transitions and animations.
- Responsive layout for different screen sizes.

## 📱 Responsive Design

PaisaFlow is designed with a mobile-first approach and adapts to different screen sizes, including smartphones, tablets and desktops.
